Head-Start Kanji Support

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Head-Start Kanji is a quiz app for learning the readings, writing and meanings of kanji, from first grade through junior high school entrance exams.

Contact

Please send bug reports, comments and requests to the email address below. You can also create an email from "Feedback to the author" in the app's Parent settings.

Frequently asked questions

  • My purchased grade is locked again / I changed devices: Tap "Restore purchases" in Parent settings. You need to be signed in with the same store account you used to buy.
  • Notifications do not arrive: Allow notifications for this app in your device settings. Focus modes or battery-saving settings may block or delay them.
  • I want to delete my study records: Use "Reset all study history" in Parent settings. Records are stored only on your device, so deleting the app also deletes them.
  • I want to change the language: Use the globe icon at the top right of Home, or "Language" in Parent settings. Questions stay in Japanese.
